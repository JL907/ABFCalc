How the math works
  • At 9 PM the outlets go dead. A battery with charge and no charged battery above it starts draining.
  • It loses 1 charge every 5 s for each device below it, plus 1 for each battery below it. Every downstream battery and device counts, even through plug strips and other batteries.
  • Downstream batteries hold their charge until the batteries above them are empty. That's why long chains are wasteful and why one battery feeding a strip of batteries works well.
  • Plug strips, plug boards and levers add no load. A lever set to Off cuts off everything after it.
  • The parts list comes from the wiki's deployables page. The wiki doesn't mark which items need power, so a few may not actually draw any. Search for anything missing and add it as a custom device.
  • Every device drains 1 by default. The wiki says some devices (like a refrigerator) drain more than others but doesn't give numbers, so change a device's drain if you've measured it.
  • Lasers: the Laser Emitter costs its battery 1 per 5 s. A Laser Power Converter at the end of the beam acts like an outlet that stays on all night while the emitter has power, so nothing after it adds load to the emitter's battery. Batteries under a converter recharge. Prisms split one beam into three, and reflectors pass the beam on. The wiki has no numbers for this; it's based on player reports.
  • Numbers come from the Abiotic Factor wiki, and the planner matches its worked examples (2 Makeshift in series = 375 s for one device, 2 Industrial = 291.67 s for three). This is a fan-made tool.
